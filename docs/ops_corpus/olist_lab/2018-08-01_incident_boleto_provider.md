---
title: "Incident INC-502: boleto provider outage"
type: incident
date: 2018-08-01
---
The boleto (bank slip) payment provider changed its API without notice. Many boleto slips generated in August could not be paid by customers, and unpaid orders were automatically canceled after the payment window expired.

Credit card, debit card and voucher payments are not affected. Workaround: prompt boleto customers to switch to card payment.
