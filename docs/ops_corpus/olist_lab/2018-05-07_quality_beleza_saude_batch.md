title: "Quality alert: faulty batch from a health & beauty supplier"
type: quality_alert
date: 2018-05-07

A large supplier in the beleza_saude (health & beauty) category shipped a batch of products with broken seals and leaking containers. Customers who received items from this batch in May are leaving 1-star reviews.

Action: remove the batch from sale, offer refunds, and monitor the review score for the category.
