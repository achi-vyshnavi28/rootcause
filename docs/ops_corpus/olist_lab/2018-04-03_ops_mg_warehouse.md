---
title: "Ops update: Belo Horizonte fulfilment hub paused"
type: ops_update
date: 2018-04-03
---
The fulfilment hub serving Minas Gerais (MG) is paused for three weeks for a flood-damage repair. During this period checkout shows longer delivery estimates for MG postcodes and several marketplace sellers temporarily hid their listings for MG.

Expect lower order volume from MG customers in April. Other states are served from Sao Paulo as usual.
