---
title: "SOP: investigating a rise in late deliveries"
type: sop
---
1. Confirm the metric: late = delivered after order_estimated_delivery_date, among delivered orders only.
2. Break down by customer state, seller state and seller to see whether the rise is regional or seller-specific.
3. Check carrier incident logs for the same weeks.
4. If one region dominates, contact the regional carrier manager before changing delivery promises.
