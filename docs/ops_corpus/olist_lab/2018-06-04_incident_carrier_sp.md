---
title: "Incident INC-418: carrier capacity shortage in Sao Paulo"
type: incident
date: 2018-06-04
---
Our main last-mile carrier in the state of Sao Paulo (SP) lost around a third of its drivers after a contract dispute. Parcels to SP customers are being delivered about a week later than promised.

Mitigation: a second carrier is being onboarded; expected recovery in July. Customer support has a macro for late-delivery complaints.
